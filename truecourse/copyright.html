Our material

The lessons, illustrations, quiz questions and examinations in TrueCourse are original works and are protected by copyright. They are licensed to you for your personal study only.

What you may not do

You may not reproduce, distribute, publish or sell the course material, and you may not use it to teach a paying audience, without written permission.

Regulatory source material

Course material summarises and explains publications issued by the UK Civil Aviation Authority and other authorities. Those publications remain the property of their respective authorities, and TrueCourse claims no ownership of them. Where material is quoted, it is quoted for the purpose of instruction and criticism, and the source is identified.

Reporting an infringement

If you believe material in TrueCourse infringes your copyright, contact us at support@zerobatics.com with a description of the work, where it appears in the app, and your contact details. The full notice-and-takedown procedure must be completed before publication.
